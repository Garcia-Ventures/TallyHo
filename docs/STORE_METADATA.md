# Google Play Store Metadata & Graphic Specifications

## 1. Store Listing Copy

### App Name

`Tally Ho - Digital Scorekeeper`

### Short Description (max 80 chars)

`Your digital pencil & paper for game night. Quick, warm, and paper-simple scorekeeping.`

### Full Description (max 4000 chars)

```text
Tally Ho is your digital pencil & paper for game night! Designed to eliminate the friction of scorekeeping—searching for a working pen, doing mental math under pressure, or losing track of who is winning—so friends and family can focus on the fun, rivalry, and connection of playing games together.

FEATURES:
• Game Presets: Instant setup for card and board games like Rummy (500), Uno, Pass the Pigs, Qwirkle, and custom matches.
• Paper-Style Aesthetics: Tactile UI with warm paper tones, charcoal slate ink, and fluid animations.
• Universal Scoring Models: Supports Race to High target, Race to Low target, and Fixed Rounds.
• Live Leaderboard & Round Logs: Real-time standings with one-tap editing to fix mistaken entries easily.
• Victory Celebrations: Animated winner podiums and match summary highlights.
• 100% Offline & Private: No ads, no sign-ups required, and zero intrusive tracking. All game data stays on your device.

Ditch the scrap paper and keep game night moving with Tally Ho!
```
