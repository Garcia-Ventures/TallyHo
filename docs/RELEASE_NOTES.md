# TallyHo User-Facing Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 (Initial Release)

### 🎲 Welcome to TallyHo!

TallyHo is your digital pencil & paper for game night! Designed to eliminate scorekeeping friction—no more hunting for working pens, doing mental math under pressure, or losing track of who is winning—so you can focus on the fun, rivalry, and connection of playing games together.

### 🌟 Highlights & What's New:

- **Instant Game Presets**: Start playing immediately with built-in templates for Rummy (500), Uno, Pass the Pigs, Qwirkle, or set up a custom match in seconds.
- **Tactile Paper Aesthetics**: Enjoy a warm, paper-style design with charcoal ink tones and fluid card flip animations.
- **Fast Score Keypad**: Enter round scores effortlessly with an oversized numeric keypad built for quick turn handoffs.
- **Live Standing & Highlights**: Track real-time leaderboards, high score streaks, and round-by-round logs with simple one-tap score editing.
- **Victory Celebrations**: Celebrate game night winners with animated podium highlights and confetti!
- **100% Private & Offline**: Works completely offline. No forced account sign-ups, no social logins, and zero intrusive tracking. All game data stays on your device.
- **Ad-Free Option**: Enjoy non-intrusive gameplay or unlock the lifetime "Remove Ads" upgrade tier for $1.99.

Happy Gaming! 🎯
